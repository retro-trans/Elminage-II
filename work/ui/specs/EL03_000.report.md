# EL03_000 UI texture report (Elminage II)

Sheet: `ANIME/EL03_000.GIM` (512x512, 8-bit palette). Spec: `work/ui/specs/EL03_000.json` (559 erase ops, 95 labels).
The spec is generated by a scratch script. Preview: `work/ui/preview/EL03_000.png`. Build output: `work/build/img/EL03_000.GIM`.
Image size, palette and GIM layout are unchanged.

Japanese texts found: 95 (each state or copy of a plate counted separately). Translated: 95. Unreadable or unfit: 0.

Left as they are (not Japanese): NAME / PARTY / EVENT LIST / TOPICS LIST / ITEM LIST / ITEM INFO / RECORDED HISTORY / ART GALLERY bars,
the record pieces ": ( ) No TITLE No GP NAME", the 1-5 number plates, the two 5x5 plates, digits 0-19, X:/Y:, NAME / LV LV plates,
the arrows, the legend icons, the Monster Alert icons, the 3D boxes and the vertical bars.

## Method
- Font: the game's 12px font (FONT12_12) everywhere. Its capitals are 7px tall, with descenders 1px lower. The 16px font is 14px
  tall and does not fit the 10-12px plate interiors, so it is not used, not even for Monster Alert.
  Spacing is 1 where the text fits and 0 where it does not (noted per row).
  Each label is placed by its rendered ink (`align: left` with an exact rect), so right/centre alignment is pixel exact.
- Colours are sampled from the original text:
  - option/plate text 247,239,236
  - value plates 255,255,255
  - pink record pieces 245,217,186
  - legend 250,220,195
  - Fame/Search 250,231,219 with a 23,12,13 outline (the original has a 1px dark outline)
  - Monster Alert 240,240,240 with a 33,36,37 outline
- Option-name column (x0-119, 22 plates of 12 rows from y66): all plates use the same art. The SE Volume plate (shortest text) was
  cleaned in four steps:
  1. copy:40 over the flat interior (x41-105, rows y91-100);
  2. built a per-pixel template (the mode of all 22 plates, plus the flat interior);
  3. re-blitted 10 glyph/AA pixels on the separator and right-gradient columns one by one, each from a plate that is clean there;
  4. blitted the whole plate to the other 21 rows.
  English is right-aligned with its last ink column at x105, the original right edge. Max 97px (x9-105).
- HP/AC plates (y66, y114) and the Face Load plate (x16,y370) are the same art as the option plates; verified identical outside
  the text. Their text area is blitted from the clean SE Volume plate. Right edges: x225 and x121.
- Value plates (72x12; black = normal, red = selected; columns x120 / x192 / x264): every plate of a colour is the same art
  (checked against the per-pixel mode of all 18 plates). One reference per colour was cleaned with copy: of its flat centre
  column (local x13; the centre band x13-60 is flat per row): black x120,y90 and red x120,y138. Each reference was then blitted
  to the other plates of its colour. English is centred on the plate (centre +36). Max 64px.
- Title bar (Party Panel Settings, y55-65): the bar darkens towards the middle. The text area x94-187 was copied from the clean
  dark column x92, including the bottom border row y65, which the glyphs touched. English is centred at x141 (original centre).
- Records header plates (y31-40): each plate's text span was copied from a dark column of the same or the neighbouring plate
  (x46, x178, x306, x360, x412, x468). The bow-tie dividers and swirl ornaments are untouched. English is centred on the
  original word, between the dividers.
- Achievement plate (x0-71, y43-52): copy:59 / copy:62 (clean gap columns). Caps kept.
- Pink record pieces 周目 (x89-112) and 状況 (x172-190), on transparent: filled transparent and redrawn at the same left edge.
- Map legend (text on transparent, 2-row bar under each entry):
  - the text rows (10 per entry) were filled transparent;
  - each bar pixel touched by a glyph was re-blitted from an entry whose bar is clean at that column, using the per-pixel mode of
    three groups: left short bars (k0-8), left long bars (k9-13) and right bars;
  - English starts at x357 (left) and x455 (right), drawn 1px higher than the original so descenders stay off the bar.
- Monster Alert frame interior (x5-76, rows 334-345): every row was filled with its own background colour, which is flat
  per row; the bevel and frame are kept.
- Fame / Search headings (on transparent): filled transparent. The 2x3 px of the サ stroke that overlapped the purple panel
  (x278-279, y455-457) were restored with copy:277.

## Verification
- Erase-only render (`work/ui/scratch_EL03_000_erase.json`, same ops, no labels):
  - zoomed crops of every region were inspected;
  - no Japanese pixels remain;
  - all plates, separators, caps, bars and the purple panel are intact.
- Bright-pixel count (>=200) is 0 inside every former Japanese box. The exceptions are boxes that also contain kept English or
  icons: 5x5, digits, X:/Y:, and the legend icons.

## Risks / in-game checks
- The sprite UV rects are not known. Every label stays inside its plate or bar, but some go past the original word:
  - "Run" (x90-111) and "Stat" (x172-197) are wider than the original 2-glyph pieces. They stay in the gap before the fixed "No"
    pieces (x121, x202). Check for clipping in the records list.
  - Legend texts were checked against the original glyph spans:
    - left short entries reach x414 at most ("Entrance"; the bar fades at x401-415);
    - the long entries end by x479 ("No Entry by Teleport", spacing 0; the long bar runs to x495);
    - right-column entries reach x510 at most ("Conveyor"; the sheet edge is x511).
    If the game cuts the legend text to the original word width, long entries will be clipped.
  - Some labels go past the original word but stay on clean plate background:
    - "Current Data" spans ink x41-125 (original x51-114). It stays between the swirl ornaments.
    - "Party Panel Settings" spans x73-208 (original x95-186). It is centred on the bar, between the ornaments (x44 / x240).
  - Option names start further left than some short originals (e.g. "Nicknames" at x36 vs 29; "Adventurer SE" at x10). All stay
    inside the plate interior (x8-105). If the game draws only part of the plate, check rows with long text.
- The 12px font is smaller than the original 10-12px Japanese glyphs. This is the same trade-off as Elminage III.
- "Hurt Voice" (やられボイス) keeps the Elminage III wording, but HELP 315 calls the setting the "hurt SE". Align one of the two if
  the HELP wording is preferred.
- The pink pieces (Run / Stat) and the legend are drawn on transparent pixels. The anti-aliased edges are quantised to the
  sheet palette, so they may look slightly darker in game than the original soft-alpha glyphs.
