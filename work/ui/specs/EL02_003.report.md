# EL02_003: battle commands, skill plates, treasure-chest and shop/bag buttons

Spec: `work/ui/specs/EL02_003.json`. Preview: `work/ui/preview/EL02_003.png`. Build output: `work/build/img/EL02_003.GIM`
(`PYTHONUTF8=1 python tools/img_text.py work/ui/specs/EL02_003.json`).

There are 27 distinct Japanese labels on 81 sprites, every label in 3 states (selected / normal / disabled). All 81 are translated. The layout differs from Elminage III: Elm2 has the six skill plates (Dispel ... Breath) and has no Disclaimer plates or small bag plates.

|---|---|---|---|

The gap rows between sprites are transparent. Under and over the selected sprites they held the glow of the Japanese text, including rows 378-380 and 392-395 around the skull icon strip.

## Method
For each group and state, one reference plate is cleaned, then fully `blit:`ed onto the other plates of that state. Ambush gets only rel x0-62, so its own right edge stays.
- Battle, reference Attack (y0):
  - Normal / disabled: rows 1-16, rel x4/5/6-29 are copied from the clean column rel x31, following the shape of the left frame.
  - Selected: the red glow of the text spreads about 5 px around it, so rows 0-15 rel x4/5/6/8-35 are copied from rel x36, and rows 16-17 from rel x50 (the vine starts at rel x34 on those rows).
- Skill plates, reference Steal (y180):
  - Rows 3-16 (body) rel x7/8-33 are copied from rel x34. Each body row has one RGBA value, translucency included.
  - Rows 0-2 above the body (glyph tops, plus the glow on the selected plate) are cleared to alpha 0. The top line of the bracket on row 2 is continued to rel x9.
  - The inner edge of the left bracket (rel x5-7, rows 11-17) had been touched by the glyph outline. It is blitted from the Breath plate (y240), whose text starts further right.
- Chest, reference Open (y340):
  - Normal / disabled: rows 0-12 rel x16-53, rows 13-14 except the line art, from rel x60. The vine peak (rel x51-53, rows 13-14) is restored by copying its neighbouring line pixels. The bump at rel x38-40 is kept. The row-15 line under the first glyph is refilled.
  - Selected: rows 0-16 rel x16-59 are copied from rel x60 (text and glow).
- Shop, reference Servant's Bag (y436): its text covers the vine. Its rel x0-67 is identical art to the chest plate, so that part is blitted from the cleaned chest reference of the same state. The selected plate also gets rel x68-73 (glow) from rel x75.
- Glow outside the plates: every gap row in the selected columns is cleared (`fill:0,0,0,0`), and so are rows 392-395 x12-171 (over the shop row; the icon triangles at x5-10 and x177-182 are kept). On row 380, the top row of the skull icons, the glow lay between the skull ears at x14-67. Those pixels are copied from the same skull phase 60 px to the right, and the ears are untouched.
- Text: 16 px game font, left-aligned, spacing 1 with spacing 0 as fallback, 1 px outline. Colours were sampled from the source glyphs:

  | group | selected (fill / outline) | normal | disabled |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | battle | 255,255,255 / 125,39,30 | 253,245,240 / 4,7,8 | 102,102,102 / 17,17,17 |
  | skill plates | 253,245,240 / 95,70,39 | 253,245,240 / 3,3,3 | 102,102,102 / 20,15,12 |
  | chest, shop | 255,255,255 / 126,31,21 | 252,222,185 / 38,13,8 | 102,102,102 / 32,17,9 |

  Text on the translucent skill plates is opaque (alpha 255), like the source glyphs.
- Verification: an erase-only render (labels removed, quantised to the sheet palette) shows clean plates, and every reference text area is uniform per row. All gap rows are alpha 0, apart from the gradient bars and the icon triangles.

### Labels wider than their own Japanese word
1st Strike, Breath, Inspect (chest), Disarm Trap, Spell Insp., Spell Unlock, Servant's Bag, Split Gold and Pool Gold.

All of them stay inside the area already used by the longest Japanese label of the same plate type (skill plates to rel x70, shop to rel x104), with two exceptions:
- Disarm Trap ends at rel x88.
- Spell Unlock ends at rel x89 (outline).

The widest chest label in Japanese (呪文で開錠) ended at about rel x83, so both run about 5 px further right, up to the first column of the right swirl.

## Risks and in-game checks
- The selected Japanese labels had a soft glow, red on the battle and chest/shop plates and brown on the skill plates. The English selected labels have no glow. Check that the highlight still reads in battle and at chests.
- Skill, Tarot and Barrier plates are translucent sprites, and their in-game tint is unknown. The English copies the source contrast (opaque text on a translucent body). Check them in battle.
- "1st Strike" and "Spell Insp." are abbreviations. "Strip" follows the JOBABL short form. "Guard" (Elm2 help text) differs from Elm3's "Defend".
- On the selected battle plate, the sheen left of the text (rows 0-2 and 15-17) was glow-affected and is now flat. This is barely visible at 1x.
