# EL01_001 - skill/ability name plates

Spec: `work/ui/specs/EL01_001.json`. Preview: `work/ui/preview/EL01_001.png`. Output: `work/build/img/EL01_001.GIM`
(same size and palette as the source; 263460 bytes).

Texts found: 47 (45 plates 136x16 + 2 small status plates). Translated: 47. Kept: the VALUABLES LIST plate (already
English, its faint low-alpha shadow pixels too), the arrow and frame pieces, the red bar, the black boxes, and the whole
lower half (boosted-contrast check: blank bars, frames, icons, no text).

## Layout
- Plates are 136x16 in three columns (A x0, B x136, C x272), row pitch 16: A0-A15, B0-B15, C0-C12. Blue: all of A,
  B0-B8 and C11. Green: B9-B15 and C0-C12 except C11. (The colours differ from Elminage III, where most of A/B is green.)
- Every plate of one colour has the same background texture (pairwise diffs are text plus a few 1-px palette dithers).
- Small dark-red status plates: region 4 (409,81 76x15) and region 5 (409,103 76x15). Interior x420-472, rows 83-94 /
  105-116. The top line is dark grey, the bottom line a gold gradient, and the caps are olive.

## Method
- Reference plates: A5 (blue, 0,80, 鍛冶) and C5 (green, 272,80, 古式). Two-character plates have their text only at rel x 52-85.
  Each one is cleaned with plateau texture: rel x 48-67 from rel x 24-43 and rel x 68-89 from rel x 90-111, all 16 rows.
  A12 was not used because it has a stray glyph pixel on its bottom border (rel 40,15).
- Every other plate is replaced whole (136x16, caps and border rows included) by a blit of its colour's reference.
  This also removes the glyph shadows that touch border rows 0 and 15.
- Small plates: the interior is refilled row by row with the sampled background colours (black, dark red, red, dark red, brown-black,
  black). This gives a smooth version of the mottled original, which is almost fully covered by the glyphs. The top line
  (rows 82/104) is redrawn grey 37,39,38. Glyph feet on the gold bottom line (x430-442 and 456-457) are restored by comparing the
  two plates: they differ where one plate's glyph touches, and 456-457 dip in both. The low-alpha glyph shadow pixels above the
  plates are cleared: row 81 x425/426/453/454/465/466, row 103 x453/454/465/466. The frame corner pixels at x419/474 are kept.
- Text: plates use the 16 px game font, white, centred, no outline, inset 16 (rect = plate x+3, 130 wide). The font falls back to spacing 0 when
  the text is wider than 114 px. Small plates use the 12 px font, spacing 0, colour 235.
- Verified with an erase-only render (spec with labels removed). Every plate is clean, both small plates are clean, and no pixel outside
  the plate block / small plates changed. The bright neutral pixels left in the block are the caps, the light bottom border row and the
  English VALUABLES LIST. Final render: no label overflow error.

## Open points / in-game checks
- 一閃 "Flash" (C12) differs from the budget-forced JOBABL/EBOOT "Hew". Align the data cell once its size is known, or switch
  the plate to "Hew".
- つばめ返し "Swallow Cut" (C9) follows the Elm2 bestiary (MON_TEXT 342:1). Elm3 used "Swallow Return". Add the chosen form
  to the glossary.
- 楽器演奏 "Instrument Playing" follows Elm2 slice 066 (Elm3: "Play Instrument").
- New wordings to confirm: 首切率上昇 "Decapitation Up", 物理攻撃威力UP "Phys Attack Up", 武器破壊/防具破壊 "Wpn Brk"/"Arm Brk".
- 16 px descenders (p, g, y) reach plate row 15 (the light bottom border) by 1 px, and cap tops sit on row 1, as in Elm3.
  No outline is drawn because a 1 px outline would spill into the next plate.
- The original text was white-to-silver with a dark shadow. The plain white reads well on the blue/green plates in the
  preview, but check legibility on hardware.
- UV rects are unknown. All plate labels stay inside their 136x16 plate. "Dungeon Tea Party" is wider than the Japanese
  (x+8..x+128), but still inside the plate interior.
- The small-plate interior is now a smooth row gradient instead of the mottled texture. Only a few pixels show between the letters.
