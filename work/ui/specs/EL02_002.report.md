# EL02_002: menu buttons (temple, castle, training hall, camp, system)

Spec: `work/ui/specs/EL02_002.json`. Preview: `work/ui/preview/EL02_002.png`. Build output: `work/build/img/EL02_002.GIM`
(`PYTHONUTF8=1 python tools/img_text.py work/ui/specs/EL02_002.json`).

There are 25 distinct Japanese labels on 84 sprites. Every label appears in 3 states (selected / normal / disabled), and Save / Suspend / Options appear twice. All 84 are translated. Nothing else on the sheet has Japanese; the rest is transparent.

## Layout
The layout is the same as Elminage III's EL02_002. The colour scheme differs: silver selected plate, dark red normal plate, dark disabled plate.
- Group A: 114x18 plates. Columns: selected x0, normal x114, disabled x228. Rows y = 20r (r 0-14).
- Column x342, rows 0-8: Save / Suspend / Options with their 3 states stacked vertically (selected, normal, disabled). The plate art is pixel-identical to group A, apart from the text.
- Group B: 96x18 plates. Columns x0 / x96 / x192, rows y 300-480.
- Text area (plate-relative): starts at rel x16 (the left swirl ends at x15). It ends before the right swirl: rel x103 for A, rel x88 for B. The bottom line art (row 15, a bump at rel x38-42, a vine from rel x49) runs under the text.
- The 2 px gap under each plate (rows y+18, y+19) is transparent. Under the selected plates it held the soft glow of the Japanese text.

## Method
- Reference plates: Pray (y20) for A and C, Back (y420) for B.
  - Selected: the background is a flat vertical gradient, and the text glow spreads about 5 px around the glyphs. Rows 0-16, rel x16-49 are replaced by a clean column (`copy:` rel x58, past the vine).
  - Normal / disabled (no glow): rows 0-13, rel x16-44 are copied from rel x45; row 14 only up to rel x37, to keep the bump. On row 15 the glyph shadow that cut the bottom line (rel x17/18-21) is filled with the line colour (`copy:` rel x16/17). The bump at rel x38-42 and the vine stay untouched.
- Every other plate of the same state, including the x342 column, is fully `blit:`ed from the cleaned reference (114x18 or 96x18).
- Gap rows under and over the selected plates (x0-113, x0-95 and x342-455) are cleared to transparent (`fill:0,0,0,0`). This removes the Japanese-shaped glow.
- Text: 16 px game font, left-aligned. Glyph left edge at rel x17 (inset 2), or rel x16 when inset is 0. Spacing 1, with spacing 0 as fallback. 1 px outline. Colours were sampled from the source glyphs:
  - Selected: fill 255,255,255, outline 134,44,35
  - Normal: fill 252,222,184, outline 31,17,9
  - Disabled: fill 102,102,102, outline 31,17,9
- Verification: an erase-only render (labels removed, quantised to the sheet palette) shows clean plates. Every plate equals its state reference. The text area of each reference is uniform per row (no stray glyph pixels). All gap rows have alpha 0.

## Risks and in-game checks
- The selected Japanese labels had a soft dark-red glow, both on the plate and in the 2 px gap below it. The English selected labels have a crisp 1 px outline and no glow. Check that the selected button still reads as highlighted.
- The UV rects are unknown. If the game samples the 2 px gap rows (now transparent under the selected plates, as they already were under the normal and disabled ones), nothing visible changes.
- On the left of the selected reference, rel x12-15 (the right edge of the left swirl) still carries the faint glow. This is invisible at 1x.
- "Roster" and "Audience" differ from the HELP_TEXT menu names ("List", "Seek Audience"). Align them if the reviewer prefers. "Seek Audience" would only fit with squeeze, which drops the outline.
