# EL03_002: compass art and bars (UI texture report, Elminage II)

Sheet: `ANIME/EL03_002.GIM` (512x512, 8-bit palette). **No Japanese text was found, so there is no spec.** `EL03_002.json` is
left out on purpose, and the sheet stays untouched.

## What was checked
- The RGBA render on a magenta background, at 2x.
- An 8x brightness boost of the RGB.
- The alpha channel.
- The palette indices of every dark bar sprite. `work/ui/regions/EL03_002.json` lists sprites 19-49 at x0-336, y282-509.
  Every interior row of each bar is a single palette index, which leaves no room for hidden glyphs. The top row is a 1-2 index
  edge and the bottom row is a highlight line; alpha is 244/255.

## Contents
- 3 large ornate compass discs (y0-160).
- 7 large and 8 small orbs (dark to cyan, gauge states).
- 2 square orange frames.
- 3 radial glow rings.
- About 30 flat dark bars and plates of various widths, with no text.

The sheet differs from Elminage III's EL03_002: it has no "COMPASS" title bars. It holds no English or Japanese text.
